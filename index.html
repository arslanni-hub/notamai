Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as one briefing?
Each route or airport query generates one briefing. KJFK EGLL and KJFK alone each count as one. Free accounts include 3 briefings per month; Pro and Max accounts have unlimited briefings subject to fair use.
What is NOTAM Intelligence Chat?
NOTAM Intelligence Chat is a general aviation AI assistant — separate from your briefing. Ask it anything: regulations, procedures, aircraft systems, airspace, navigation. It draws on live web search when information may have changed recently. Available on Pro and Max plans.
How does AI Video Briefing work?
Enter a two-airport route and NOTAM Intelligence generates a fully narrated video briefing — risk assessment, decoded NOTAMs, weather summary and Go/No-Go recommendation, delivered as a short video you can watch before departure. Available on Max (5 videos/month), with additional video packs available for purchase.
How do NOTAM Alerts work?
Add any airport or FIR to your watch list and you'll receive an email notification the moment a new NOTAM is issued for that location. Available on Pro and Max plans.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel your subscription anytime with no cancellation fees.
Is my data secure?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never share your flight data with third parties.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
Enter your credit card to start. You won't be charged for 7 days. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and pay nothing.
What does the Enterprise plan include?
The Enterprise plan supports up to 10 users under a single workspace at $999/month, with full access to all Max features plus API access, priority support with SLA, dedicated account management, and custom onboarding. For teams larger than 10 users, contact us at support@notamai.com for custom pricing.
